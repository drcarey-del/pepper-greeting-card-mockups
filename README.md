# Pepper the Dog Greeting Card Mockups

Faire catalog product mockups for Melon Glow Books / Pepper the Dog.

**Style locked in:**
- White embossed cardstock
- 4x6 photo mounted with clear half-inch white border
- Subtle embossed line around the photo
- Kraft belly band on the stack of 6 closed cards (title large in clean serif + usage suggestions smaller)
- Open card standing beside the pack showing the full front
- Usage line ends with **Anytime** (instead of Just Because) so retailers know the card works for planned occasions and spontaneous messages
